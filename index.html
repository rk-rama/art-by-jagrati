About the Artist

Hi, I'm Jagrati. I have been drawing for as long as I can remember, first in the margins of school notebooks, then on canvas. Over the years I moved from pencil to charcoal, and from watercolor to oils, always chasing the same thing: a picture that feels like the person or place it shows.

Every commission is hand-drawn and hand-painted. No filters, no prints. I talk with you about the story behind the piece, send progress photos along the way, and finish only when it feels right to both of us.

Portraits of family, a gift for an anniversary, a landscape for a blank wall: tell me what you have in mind.
